Full Equation

Let the workplace population be a finite set S = {1, 2, ..., N} partitioned into subsets: SBM, SFT, and SPT.

W = ∑i ∈ S wi = 3|SBM| + 2|SFT| + 1|SPT|
Pi = T 2N + wi · T 2 · W

T ≡ Total gross tip pool (£)

N ≡ Total eligible staff population

W ≡ Aggregate sum of weighted role points

wi ≡ Discrete coefficient (BM: 3, FT: 2, PT: 1)

Pi ≡ Net dividend for worker i
