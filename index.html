Full Equation

Let the workplace population be a finite set S = {1, 2, ..., N} partitioned into three mutually disjoint subsets: SBM, SFT, and SPT.

W = ∑i ∈ S wi = 3|SBM| + 2|SFT| + 1|SPT|
Pi = T 2N + wi · T 2 · W
Pi = T 2 · [ 1 N + wi 3nBM + 2nFT + nPT ]

T ≡ Total gross tip capital (£)

N ≡ Total eligible staff population (|S| = nBM + nFT + nPT)

W ≡ Total weighted points across all staff members

wi ≡ Discrete role weighting coefficient for staff member i:
• wi = 3  for i ∈ SBM (Assistant Managers)
• wi = 2  for i ∈ SFT (Full Time)
• wi = 1  for i ∈ SPT (Part Time)

Pi ≡ Net individualized tip dividend for worker i
